# Sources

Every document in the register is an official Government of Assam publication (the pension checklist is from the Office of the Accountant General (A&E), Assam). For reproducibility the exact bytes used are fixed: where the file was taken from the Internet Archive's copy of the official address, both addresses are listed. Anyone can confirm a file by comparing its SHA-256 with the department's copy. In the pilot, documents come directly from the owners. Retrieved 24 November 2026.
